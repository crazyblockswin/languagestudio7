Explore, Listen & Learn

Across 84 interactive Adventures, Language Studio 7 offers 1,588 original Activity and Challenge sentences, each available in all seven supported languages. That makes 11,116 sentence versions to explore.

Discover 1,336 interactive hotspots, listen to spoken words, and practice through Activities and Challenges at your own pace. All Activity and Challenge sentences are translated into the six additional languages.

A Note About the Numbers

The sentence totals cover Adventure Activities and Challenges. Hotspot labels and other learning pages are not included in those sentence totals.

Of 8,016 possible hotspot translations, 7,972 are currently filled; 44 are missing.

Figures recalculated from the supplied published-scenes.js data on November 5, 2026. Translation totals count non-empty entries; translation quality and live-site behavior have not been independently verified.
