Choose files from your PC: Click + Add picture to browse for a JPG/JPEG. Each entry also has buttons to choose or replace its picture and MP3. The editor previews your selected local files and fills in their exact filenames.

Publishing: Because LS7 uses GitHub Pages, choosing a file here does not upload it to the website. Upload the actual JPGs to memory-lane/images/, MP3s to memory-lane/sounds/, then click Download catalog and upload the downloaded memory-lane-data.js into memory-lane/. Nothing is embedded in HTML.

Filenames must match the files uploaded to GitHub exactly, including capital letters. Use ↑ and ↓ to change the order.
